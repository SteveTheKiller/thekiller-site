Palette Experiments

Ten header treatments in the beige-plus-green cassette-futurism direction. Same wordmark, same font (Departure Mono for headers, VT323 for status text), same placeholder smiley - only the case, LCD, and framing differ. Each card names what it's riffing on. Point at any subset to develop further and I'll wire it into the actual site.

01 · WALKMAN CREAMDeparture Mono
STEVE.THE.KILLER
Sony Walkman WM-2 vibe. Cream ABS case, no LCD; deep green ink for text and smiley features. Grunge grit lives in the smiley, not the frame. Cleanest option.
02 · GAME BOY DMGDeparture Mono + VT323
STEVE.THE.KILLER
Nintendo DMG-01. Smiley outside the LCD like a case-mounted badge; wordmark in the pea-soup LCD. Inset bezel. Reads game-y - fun for a personal brand.
03 · CASIO STRIPDeparture Mono + VT323
STEVE.THE.KILLER · FIELD-TECH-KILLER-01
KILL-01
Casio calculator / early scientific instrument. Product name up top like a device label, LCD strip below with a memorable code. Smiley lives inside the LCD as a status glyph.
04 · IBM YELLOWEDDeparture Mono
STEVE.THE.KILLER
1980s IBM PC / Model M keyboard. Aged plastic beige. Only "THE" glows phosphor-green like a status LED. Subtle - hits the cassette-futurism from a serious-office angle rather than a game-y one.
05 · KRAFT J-CARDDeparture Mono
SIDE A STEVE.THE.KILLER
Cassette J-card sticker. Paper grain, "SIDE A / 60 MIN" marks, red tape strip on KILLER. Grungiest of the set. Wordmark rides low, smiley sits in the "album art" slot.
06 · CRT TUBEDeparture Mono
> STEVE.THE.KILLER_
Beige computer case bezel with a black CRT tube inside. Phosphor-green wordmark, command-prompt marker and cursor. Scanlines and vignette on the tube only. Reads as a workstation.
07 · VHS TRACKINGDeparture Mono
STEVE.THE.KILLER
Same beige-plus-green base as 01, but with a soft VHS tracking bar sliding across and a fake timecode in the corner. Subtle - looks static until you notice. Could animate the bar slowly.
08 · NOKIA MUDDYVT323
STEVE.THE.KILLER
Nokia 3310 LCD backdrop. Muddy olive-green, sharp inset bezel, VT323 body font. Cheapest-looking and most memorable. Feels like brutal, functional, nothing-precious tech.
09 · TORN ZINEDeparture Mono
STEVE.THE.KILLER
Retains the ransom-note DNA of your current wordmark but in beige. Torn edge, dropped shadow, rotated red stamp on KILLER. Least "electronic" - closest to what you have now.
10 · SPLIT DUODeparture Mono + VT323
STEVE.THE. KILLER
Half-cream / half-LCD block. Two typefaces read as a physical seam between plastic case and screen. Bright and immediate. Feels the most "1985 product" of the set.
