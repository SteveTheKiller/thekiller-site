Steve the Killer — Smiley Drafts

Six directions, brand palette (amber / blood / bone / ink). Each SVG uses named layer groups you can toggle or edit in GIMP or Inkscape.

01Slit-Smile

X eyes, gaping throat-cut mouth with teeth shards, blood drips down the face.

02Glitched-Grin

Chromatic-aberration ghosts, horizontal scanline slice through eyes, dead-pixel scatter. IT/scripting nod.

03Ransom-Face

Face parts as taped-on torn paper — matches the "Steve the Killer" wordmark's cut-and-paste letters.

04Stitched-Frankie

Mismatched face patches sewn with visible X stitches, one X eye + one dot, stitched zig-zag mouth.

05Peel-Face

Right half of the amber "skin" peeled back like a sticker, revealing a red skull with teeth underneath.

06Cracked-Porcelain

Placid smile, but the face is shattered — spider-web cracks and a missing chunk to the void.
